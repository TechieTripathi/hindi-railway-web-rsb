What do these columns mean?
source — the publisher or railway zone, the publication date, and listing: the page the article was found on. Open that first when a publisher changes their site layout. An orange * means the listing was not recorded on the article and has been guessed from the source config.
article — the title, then the opening of the body (shortened), then how it was read: chars of body text extracted (orange under 300 means extraction probably failed quietly), {% if pipeline == 'rsb' %}how much of the text is actually Devanagari — some zones publish Hindi headlines over English text, {% elif generic_expected %}which extraction rule found the text (generic is normal here: most publishers have no site-specific rule), {% else %}which extraction rule found the text (orange generic means that site's specific rule stopped working), {% endif %} and a link to the article page itself.
quality — whether the AI has written this one, and once scored: fact, the AI's own confidence; cov, the measured share of source figures it carried over; and plag, the share of the story's 4-word phrases that also appear in the source, calculated locally. Plagiarism is lower is better — green at 20% or under, orange to 35%, red above — and a dash means the texts were too short to compare. A ⚠ on fact/cov means a hard gate failed. Full definitions in research/docs/METRICS.md.
